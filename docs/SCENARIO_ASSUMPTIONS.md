# Scenario assumptions

Emerald Reach is a wholly fictional rainforest training region and does not represent a real Indigenous territory. Coordinates are used to make the included vector data spatially consistent and interactive; they are not navigational or authoritative geographic information.

The seeded grid is deterministic. Fuels, moisture, and slope are generated from the scenario seed. Spread probability combines neighboring fire, fuel, dryness, humidity, wind alignment, and a slope heuristic. Smoke is a simple decaying downwind deposition. These equations are transparent demonstrations, not calibrated environmental models.

Three aggregated community estimates and two shelter records are illustrative. Population, accessibility flags, route capacities, wind, shelter occupancy, habitat labels, and modeled fire inputs are synthetic. They must not be interpreted as measured, current, or community-approved information.

No live satellite products, real-time weather, emergency dispatch, geocoding, or public notification are integrated. The visible simulation-mode banner must remain present when using this bundled scenario.
